Privacy and safety

Sage can reach a lot of your life. Here’s exactly how it treats it.

Four rules apply to everything it does, and you can pause all of it with one tap.

1Read-only by defaultIt reads what you connect and acts only when a task you started needs it.
2Secrets stay in 1PasswordIDs, cards and codes show only after Face ID or your passcode.
3You approve spendingNothing is bought without your OK, unless you set a limit for that person.
4Only what a task needsA name and number for a booking, an address for a delivery. Bill collectors get nothing.
